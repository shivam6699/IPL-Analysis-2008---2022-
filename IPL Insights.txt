Powerplay & Death-Over Trends (2008-2022)
Powerplay (Overs 1-6)
Highest powerplay runs: 2022 (6,694 runs)
Lowest powerplay runs: 2009 (4,620 runs)
Highest powerplay wickets lost: 2022 (214 wickets)
Lowest powerplay wickets lost: 2016 (155 wickets)
Powerplay run rate trend: Increased from 13.5 (2009) to 15.07 (2022)
Death Overs (Overs 16-20)
Highest death-over runs: 2022 (5,185 runs)
Lowest death-over runs: 2007/08 (3,410 runs)
Most wickets in death overs: 2013 & 2022 (298 & 273 wickets)
Fewest wickets in death overs: 2020/21 (207 wickets)
Death-over run rate trend: Increased from 11.7 (2007) to 14.01 (2022)

Observations:
Powerplay scoring has steadily increased, but wickets lost have also risen in recent seasons.
Death-over hitting has improved, showing higher run rates in recent IPL seasons.
Teams now take more risks in the powerplay and explode in the death overs.
